## 1. Agency Overview
Brick & Beyond is a premier real estate marketing and communications agency. Our mission is to represent residential and commercial developers with uncompromising transparency, regulatory compliance, and persuasive narrative excellence.

---

## 2. Core Brand Principles

### 2.1 Brand Voice & Tone
- **Consultative & Thoughtful:** Act as trusted property advisors, never high-pressure sales hawkers.
- **Transparent & Evidentiary:** Ground every claim, specification, and timeline in verified client master records.
- **Aspirational yet Grounded:** Highlight elevated lifestyle aspirations without engaging in vulgar exaggeration or ungrounded promises.

### 2.2 Prohibited Deceptive Tactics
- **No Artificial Scarcity:** Fabricating urgency (e.g., "only 2 units left", "midnight price surge") without verified inventory records is strictly banned.
- **No Speculative Transit Claims:** Proximity times must specify realistic traffic conditions (e.g., "approx. 20 mins off-peak"). Unapproved public infrastructure must not be reported as completed.

---

## 3. Financial & Investment Communication Rules

### 3.1 Absolute Prohibition of Guaranteed Returns
- In accordance with real estate regulatory compliance and agency standards, **no marketing communication may guarantee, promise, or assure financial returns, fixed rental yields, or capital appreciation percentages**.
- Prohibited phrases: *"Guaranteed 14% Rental Yield"*, *"Assured Returns"*, *"Double your money in 3 years"*.
- Permitted alternative: *"Indicative rental yield based on current micro-market estimates; investments are subject to market risks."*

---

## 4. Architectural & Specification Standards

### 4.1 Carpet Area Primacy
- Advertised residential units must always disclose the **RERA Carpet Area** in square feet.
- Quoting prices or marketing residential configurations solely using *"Super Built-up Area"* or *"Saleable Area"* is strictly prohibited.

### 4.2 Superlative Substantiation
- Terms such as *"Largest"*, *"Best"*, *"Most Exclusive"*, or *"First-Ever"* must be supported by an official award, government survey, or architectural certificate on file.
- Without documented proof, copywriters must default to balanced descriptive terms such as *"Spacious"*, *"Well-appointed"*, or *"Contemporary"*.

---

## 5. Regulatory Disclosures
Every public advertisement across print, digital, outdoor, and social media must clearly and legibly display:
1. The developer's registered corporate name.
2. The official project title and location.
3. The valid project registration reference (e.g., `DEMO-HRERA-GGM-2026-0042`).
4. The regulatory authority portal link (`https://haryanarera.gov.in`).
